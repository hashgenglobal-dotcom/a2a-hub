## How to Log

When you see a signal:

1. Note the date and source (HN thread URL, Reddit post, etc.)
2. Quote or summarize the signal
3. Note the pattern — is this a one-off or a recurring theme?
4. Do not act on it yet. Let patterns emerge first.
