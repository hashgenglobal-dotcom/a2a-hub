## Outreach Process

1. **Research first** — read the project's README, issues, and recent activity
2. **Open an issue** — frame as a question or discussion, not a request
3. **Be specific** — reference their work, explain why you're reaching out
4. **Ask for feedback** — not promotion
5. **Log the response** — in `VALIDATION_METRICS.md`

## Expected Outcomes

| Outcome | Signal Strength | What to Do |
|---------|----------------|------------|
| "Here's our Agent Card URL" | Strong | Add to seeds, re-deploy, log submission |
| "Interesting, tell me more" | Medium | Continue the conversation |
| "No thanks" | Neutral | Thank them and move on |
| No response | Neutral | Follow up once after 1 week, then move on |
